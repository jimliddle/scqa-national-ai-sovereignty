Source directory and funding notes

Sources and methodology · Reviewed 9 October 2026

See Review & Coverage for corrections and gaps. Source dates vary. Announcements retain their original date; expected completion is not treated as delivery. This review corrects the April edition and checks material developments through 9 October 2026. Coverage is selective.

Scores use a 1–10 scale: 1–3 early capability, 4–6 developing, 7–8 strong and 9–10 leading within this sample. They reflect editorial judgment. The Posture Scorer shows the inputs, weights and calculation; Review & Coverage explains score changes. Radar and heatmap values use the same programme averages as the default scorer. Model capability and control are assessed separately in Models & Control; capability does not enter the six-factor programme score directly.

Investment figures retain original currencies and scopes. Public allocations, private ambitions and total project budgets are not directly comparable or summed. “Not stated” means no comparable verified amount is asserted. The EU overlaps with France; do not add their figures.

UK funding scope: £500M is the Sovereign AI fund envelope, not a verified disbursement total, new installed compute, or a dedicated frontier training budget. The reviewed evidence does not establish that the whole amount is new incremental spending. Official fund scope ↗.
